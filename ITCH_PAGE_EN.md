# Elemental Shogi / 属性将棋

**A new Shogi strategy game where Fire, Water, and Wind change the value of every piece in every match.**

Elemental Shogi keeps the movement, promotion, and captured-piece drops of traditional Shogi while giving every piece one of three elements. The elemental layout changes at the start of each game.

- Fire beats Wind
- Wind beats Water
- Water beats Fire

An advantageous attack captures normally. An attack at a disadvantage is countered, and the attacking piece joins the opponent's pieces in hand. Matching elements create a clash that blocks the square and starts a fight over strong-element reinforcements.

Kings have durability, creating new ways to attack and defend. Even with the familiar Shogi setup, changing elemental combinations give each match a different tactical shape.

## How to Play

Select one of your pieces, then select a highlighted destination. To drop a captured piece, select it from **Your Pieces in Hand** on the right side of the board.

New players should start with the interactive tutorial shown at launch. It lets you play through advantageous, disadvantageous, and matching-element attacks, reinforcements, and attacks against the King.

English piece notation:

K King · R Rook · B Bishop · G Gold · S Silver · N Knight · L Lance · P Pawn
