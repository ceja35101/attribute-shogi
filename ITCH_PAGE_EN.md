# Elemental Shogi / 属性将棋

**A Japanese chess variant where Fire, Water, and Wind change every battle.**

Elemental Shogi keeps the movement, promotion, and captured-piece drops of Shogi while giving every piece one of three elements:

- Fire beats Wind
- Wind beats Water
- Water beats Fire

An advantage captures normally. A disadvantage triggers a counterattack. Matching elements create a blocked clash that can be resolved by reinforcement. The element layout changes every game, so familiar pieces gain different tactical values.

## Controls

Select one of your pieces, then select a highlighted destination. Select a captured piece from your hand to drop it.

English mode uses:

K King · R Rook · B Bishop · G Gold · S Silver · N Knight · L Lance · P Pawn
